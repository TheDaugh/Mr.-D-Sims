Lessons, keys and make-up PDFs

Student page, teacher view (the same page with #key: every tab open, answers marked), and the two PDFs for lessons built from a spec. Absent students get the make-up key and copy it into their notes; they do not need to do the lesson at home.

Student IDs

Paste your roster, one student per line: Name, period (or paste two spreadsheet columns). Each ID is made from the name and period with a secret that stays in this browser, so nobody can work an ID back to a name without it. Print the cards, hand them out, and grade by ID. Download the backup and keep it somewhere safe: without it you cannot read IDs back.

Live view

Shows where each student is in the lesson page: step, level, answered, misses, times they left the page. It sees nothing outside the lesson page. It needs the small worker in live/ deployed once (see live/README.md).

Virtual labs

Open any lab to check it. The live view above does not see virtual labs: each student's work is saved in their own browser, and they hand in a record file. Load those files on the virtual-lab teacher page to see predictions, counts, attempts and CERs.

Bell schedule

Edit shared/bell.js (the SCHEDULE block) to change a period. Mon to Thu guided notes end at the 42-minute mark and lessons lock until then. On Friday there is no early end: the lesson stays locked until the bell.
