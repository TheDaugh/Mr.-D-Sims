# 3.4 Derivatives of Inverse Trigonometric Functions: outline

Self-paced, solo, no clock. Progress saves on the device. Page: `3-4-inverse-trig.html` (teacher view: add `#key`).

**Targets**
- Say what arcsin, arccos and arctan mean.
- Differentiate them with the chain rule.
- Find a slope or a tangent line.

**Flow**
1. **Warm-up** (3 easy review items): chain rule on sin(5x), differentiating sin y = x, a 3-4-5 triangle.
2. **Predict** (2): the tangent of arcsin at x = 1; what happens to the slope of arctan for large x.
3. **Explore** (4 stations, all required, in order):
   - X1 the triangle behind arcsin: hypotenuse 1, side x, third side √(1 − x²).
   - X2 how steep is arcsin: slope 1 in the middle, steeper toward ±1.
   - X3 how steep is arctan: slopes 0.5, 0.2, 0.1 at x = 1, 2, 3.
   - X4 arcsin and arccos at the same x: slopes are opposite.
   - Two observe questions.
4. **Notes**: definitions with blanks, the three rules, the chain rule version, a triangle figure to label, then a 2-question mid check.
5. **Build** (4 problems), **Practice** (endless), **Check** (2), **Wrap-up**.

**Skills** (difficulty rises with success, three hidden bands)
- `deriv`: typed derivative (chain rule inside arcsin, arccos, arctan).
- `slope`: slope at a point, clean decimals.
- `inside`: pick the correct derivative from four.
- `tangent`: typed tangent line at a nice angle.

**Common slips named by the traps:** forgetting the chain factor, forgetting to square the inside, wrong sign on arccos.
