# 3.5 Higher-Order Derivatives: outline

Self-paced, solo, no clock. Progress saves on the device. Page: `3-5-higher-order.html` (teacher view: add `#key`).

**Targets**
- Name f′, f″, f‴ and say what each tells me.
- Find a second or higher derivative with the usual rules.
- Find d²y/dx² at a point on an implicit curve.

**Flow**
1. **Warm-up** (3 easy review items): power rule, derivative of sine, what f′ > 0 means.
2. **Predict** (2): the derivative of 2x; what follows −cos x.
3. **Explore** (4 stations, all required, in order):
   - X1 the slope of the slope: f and f′ with one slider; flat tangent on f means f′ = 0, flat tangent on f′ means f″ = 0.
   - X2 match the trio: three shuffled graphs of f, f′, f″.
   - X3 the sine wheel: sin, cos, −sin, −cos repeat every 4; the 7th derivative.
   - X4 three machines: a polynomial ends at 0, e^(2x) multiplies by 2, 1/x never stops.
   - Two observe questions.
4. **Notes**: definitions with blanks, f(x) = x⁴ − 3x² + 2x worked through f‴, a three-graph figure to label, then a 2-question mid check.
5. **Build** (4 problems), **Practice** (endless), **Check** (2), **Wrap-up**.

**Skills** (difficulty rises with success, three hidden bands)
- `second`: typed f″ (polynomials, then e^(kx), sin, cos, ln, then products and chains).
- `nth`: the nth derivative at a point.
- `read`: what the sign or size of f″ says about f′.
- `implicit`: d²y/dx² at a point on a curve.

**Common slips named by the traps:** stopping at f′, one chain factor instead of two, a dropped product-rule term, sign errors in the sine cycle.
