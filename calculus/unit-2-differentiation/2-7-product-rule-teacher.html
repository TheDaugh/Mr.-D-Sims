The four paths (students never see these labels)

1 · Build. Placed here when the warm-up score is 0 to 2, or after two rough items in a row. All four pieces are scaffolded ($f$, $g$, $f'$, $g'$, then the result), with a multiply-it-out cross-check. Hint ladder of three. Contexts: small polynomials, a revenue table, a table of values.
2 · Apply. Warm-up 3 to 5. Names $f$ and $g$ and derivatives, then assembles. Trig, exponential, tangent lines, revenue rate, horizontal tangents.
3 · Stretch. Warm-up 6/6 clean, or two clean items at Apply. Gives $f$ and $g$ and asks for derivatives and result; logs, radicals, unknown functions, units-based argument, rewriting a quotient as a product.
4 · Extend. Two clean items at Stretch. Result only. Three-factor products, the second derivative $(fg)''$, deriving the quotient rule, the limit-definition proof, a related-rates volume, solving $h'=0$ with the quadratic formula.

Rules: two clean items in a row (no misses, no hints) raise the path by one. Two rough ones (three misses, or the worked solution revealed) lower it by one. Middle results hold steady. The preview link lets you open any step and pick a path yourself.

Facilitation notes

The huddle (2 to 3 minutes)

  1. Put the picture on the board: rectangle $f$ by $g$, a green strip, an orange strip, a red corner.
  2. Ask: “Which piece did each term of $f'g+fg'$ come from?” Take two answers.
  3. Ask: “Cut $\Delta t$ in half. What happens to each strip? To the corner?” (Halves, halves, quarters.) Then: “So in the limit…?”
  4. Name it: product rule. Ask someone to say it in words before they type it.

“It worked in Mission 3 so the rule is fine.”One accidental match.A rule must hold for every pair; one counterexample ends it.

For the student who is not taking the AP exam

The warm-up, scaffolds, and multiply-it-out cross-check build the process; the rectangle supplies the concept without needing a limit definition. The path moves with accuracy, so a student who is solid conceptually but shaky on mechanics lands in Build or Apply and still finishes with a printable set of notes and a check they can pass.
