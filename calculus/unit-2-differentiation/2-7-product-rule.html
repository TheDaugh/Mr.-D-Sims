2 · Play with a job

Your job: Pick a rectangle and a time step Δt, then grow it. The sim measures how fast the area grew (ΔA ÷ Δt) and splits the new area into three pieces. 4 · Name it

Call the width f and the height g. Both change, so the area f·g changes. The rule you wrote has a formal name, the product rule:

The derivative of a product is the derivative of the first times the second, plus the first times the derivative of the second.

In symbols, (f·g)′ = f′·g + f·g′. It is not f′·g′. That product is the corner, and the corner disappears in the limit.

Match the wording above. Spacing, commas and capital letters do not matter.

Not yet. Copy the statement above, word for word.

5 · Build it

The routine. It never changes.
  1. Name f and g, the two factors.
  2. Differentiate each one alone: f′ and g′.
  3. Assemble: f′·g + f·g′.
  4. Tidy up, only if the problem asks.

Each finished step stays on screen. A hint appears after each miss.

6 · Try it

Three questions. Keep going until each one is right; a hint appears after each miss.
