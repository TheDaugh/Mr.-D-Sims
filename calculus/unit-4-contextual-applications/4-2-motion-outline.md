# 4.2 Position, Velocity and Acceleration: outline

Self-paced, solo, no clock. Progress saves on the device. Page: `4-2-motion.html` (teacher view: add `#key`).

**Targets**
- Get v(t) = s′(t) and a(t) = v′(t).
- Say which way a particle moves and when it is at rest.
- Say when it speeds up or slows down (v and a same sign or not).

**Flow**
1. **Warm-up** (3 easy review items). 2. **Predict** (2).
3. **Explore** (4 stations, all required, in order), all on s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t:
   - X1 watch the particle with a velocity and an acceleration arrow: moving right, left, at rest.
   - X2 speeding up or slowing down at four moments, then the rule.
   - X3 match the three shuffled graphs of s, v, a.
   - X4 speed is the size of the velocity.
   - Two observe questions.
4. **Notes** (definitions with blanks, the worked trip, a 2-question mid check). 5. **Build**, **Practice** (endless), **Check**, **Wrap-up**.

**Skills** (three hidden bands): `vel` and `accel` (typed, polynomials, then sin/cos/exp, then products and chains), `dir` (right, left, at rest), `speed` (speeding up, slowing down, neither).

**Common slips named by the lesson**: calling negative acceleration "slowing down"; speed with a sign; at rest read as "stopped for good".
