# 4.1 Interpreting Derivatives in Context: outline

Self-paced, solo, no clock. Progress saves on the device. Page: `4-1-derivative-context.html` (teacher view: add `#key`).

**Targets**
- Say what f′(a) = v means: direction from the sign, size from the number, units "output per input".
- Give the units of f′ and f″.
- Use f(a + h) ≈ f(a) + h f′(a) to estimate a small change.

**Flow**
1. **Warm-up** (3 easy review items). 2. **Predict** (2).
3. **Explore** (4 stations, all required, in order):
   - X1 read the rate on a tank V(t) = 40 + 12t − t²: filling at 8, steady, emptying at 4.
   - X2 say it with units: three facts, pick the sentence.
   - X3 how far to trust a rate: slide h and watch the tangent estimate drift from the true value (gap h²).
   - X4 rising, falling or steady from the sign of five facts.
   - Two observe questions.
4. **Notes** (definitions with blanks, V′(3) = 6 worked, a 2-question mid check). 5. **Build**, **Practice** (endless), **Check**, **Wrap-up**.

**Skills** (three hidden bands): `meaning` (sentence or equation), `units` (f′, f″, compound), `estimate` (f(a) + h f′(a)), `rate` (find f′(a) then use it).

**Common slips named by the lesson**: giving the amount instead of the rate; units "per gallon" instead of "gallons per minute"; ignoring the sign; using the estimate for a big step.
