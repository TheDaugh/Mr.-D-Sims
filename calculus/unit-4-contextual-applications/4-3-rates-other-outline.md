# 4.3 Rates of Change in Other Contexts: outline

Self-paced, solo, no clock. Progress saves on the device. Page: `4-3-rates-other.html` (teacher view: add `#key`).

**Targets**
- Find an average rate (change ÷ width) from two values, a table or a formula.
- Find the rate right now with a derivative, with units.
- Tell "increasing, and faster" from "increasing, but slower" using f′ and f″.

**Flow**
1. **Warm-up** (3 easy review items). 2. **Predict** (2).
3. **Explore** (4 stations, all required, in order):
   - X1 average rate versus rate right now on a growing culture: shrink the width and the two numbers meet.
   - X2 bacteria B(t) and its rate B′ together.
   - X3 match three stories to three shuffled graphs.
   - X4 a table of bacteria: find five average rates, pick the greatest, say what it means.
   - Two observe questions.
4. **Notes** (definitions with blanks, a worked average and instant rate, a 2-question mid check). 5. **Build**, **Practice** (endless), **Check**, **Wrap-up**.

**Skills** (three hidden bands): `avg`, `inst`, `shape` (four shapes from the signs of f′ and f″), `say` (the average-rate sentence or equation).

**Common slips named by the lesson**: dividing by the wrong width; reading an average rate as a rate at one moment; "decreasing" for "increasing more slowly".
