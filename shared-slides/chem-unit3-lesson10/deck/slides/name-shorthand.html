Naming moment. Ask first: what could replace the 1s²2s²2p⁶ part? Let them say it, then click. The rule: the bracket is the noble gas you most recently passed, the one before the element, not the one at the end of its row. Students write [Ar]3s¹ for sodium because argon is in the same row. Check with fingers: how many electrons are inside the bracket? (10.) Gallium and chlorine come up in the worked examples.
