Predict on the sheet first: how many dots does phosphorus get, and where do they go? The animation walks across period 3 from sodium to argon, one element every few seconds, and each dot lands in the same order: the s pair on top, then the p dots go right, bottom, left one at a time, and only after those three singles do p dots pair up (sulfur is the first to pair). Tie the dot count to the valence configuration in teal. Only valence electrons get dots; the core stays out. Teachers differ on where the s pair sits; here it is on top.
