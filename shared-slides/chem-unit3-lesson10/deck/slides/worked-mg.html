Mg²⁺

Write its configuration.

1

The atom has 12 electrons.

[Ne]3s²

2

Lose 2 from the outermost shell (n = 3).

[Ne]3s² → [Ne]

3

Same as a neon atom.

Mg²⁺ = [Ne]

Protons stay 12. Only electrons move.

12 / 26

Think aloud, do not just show. Say step 2 as a decision: "Which electrons go? Not the ones that came in last. The ones farthest from the nucleus, shell 3." Click one step at a time. Students watch and do not copy: their sheet has the same three steps with blanks, so they label each step in their own words. 3 minutes.
