4s fills first. Why does it leave first?

Fills

3p → 4s → 3d

Leaves

4s → 3d

25 / 26

This one is for a student who is ready for it; do not require an answer. Short version: once the 3d electrons are present, they repel the 4s and the 4s sits in the larger shell, so it is the outermost and the easiest to remove. The orbital fill order is an energy ladder for the empty atom, not a rule about which electron leaves. If no one gets it, leave it open on purpose and say you will come back to it.
