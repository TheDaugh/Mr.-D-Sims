Honest answer: no, not cleanly. Lewis dots are built for main-group elements, where only s and p electrons take part. For transition metals, some teachers draw 2 dots for the 4s² and call it done, but that hides the 3d electrons, which matter for the ions. Say that out loud. Stretch 3 on the sheet (Fe²⁺ vs Fe³⁺) is this question made concrete. We return to transition metals in the ion and bonding lessons.
