# Effective Nuclear Charge and Shielding (Unit 3, slot 15, sim day)

**Question:** How hard does the nucleus really pull on an outer electron?
**Target (T12):** I can estimate Z_eff as Z minus core electrons and use it to compare how hard atoms hold electrons.
**Clock (42 min, from the bell):** huddle 1 at minute 3 (hold screen), huddle 2 at minute 25 (writable tab, says what is tested).

1. **Warm-up** (3 easy picture items): outer electrons of sodium, its core, Coulomb distance doubling.
2. **Predict:** lithium or sodium, which holds its outer electron harder?
3. **Explore, the Pull Meter:** mark the core rings of two atoms, predict, then read the meter. Starts at Li/Na/K, rises with success to all of period 3. More pairs than the time allows.
4. **Huddle 2:** core, shielding, Z_eff; sodium worked with blanks; label the atom; three crux questions.
5. **Models:** lithium (watch), sodium (with Mr. D), chlorine (your turn), five moves each.
6. **Build, Practice:** Z_eff for new atoms, endless at the student's hidden band.
7. **Check:** three exit items.
8. **Notes:** study sheet from the student's own work. No stamp, no report code. Scores stay on the student's device.

**On paper:** worksheet, 30 points, scales printed. Ends with "what does this predict about atom size?"

**Not taught today:** the first-ionization dips (slot 17), Cr and Cu, transition-metal ions.
