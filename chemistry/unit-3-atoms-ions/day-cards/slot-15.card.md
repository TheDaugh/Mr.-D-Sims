**Big question:** How hard does the nucleus really pull on an outer electron?
**Mr. D shows:** Z_eff = Z − core for lithium. **Students estimate:** sodium and potassium, then across period 3. **Ends with:** what does this predict about atom size?

Clock: 42 min from the bell. Huddle 1 minute 3 (hold). Huddle 2 minute 25 (writable, states what is tested). Tab order: Warm-up, Predict, Explore, Huddle, Models, Build, Practice, Check (+ Notes).

Bands (hidden): 1 Li/Na/K. 2 adds Be/Mg/Ca, F/Cl, Na to S. 3 all pairs Z = 3 to 20 with no dip pairs (the regular-level standard).

Models: M1 Li watch, M2 Na with me (types core and Z_eff), M3 Cl you do (all five moves). Slips: counting the outer electron as core; using Z as the pull; subtracting only ring 1.

Interpretations I made (see the key): pull = Z_eff ÷ n²; dips excluded; the Pull Meter's mark, predict, meter order; no projector page.
