# Slot 15 board script: the notes huddle (the middle of the lab)

**Board:** chem board, `chemistry/whiteboard/index.html#lesson=zeff`. The pictures are drawn; you write into the blanks. Select an orange cover and press Delete to show the answer. Stencils: the **Z_eff** shelf. This is the one huddle of the Pull Bench lab (a virtual lab), so students copy into **seven blanks** on their screens, in this order.

**Before the huddle:** say nothing. Students are measuring atoms on their own. No huddle at minute 3 or minute 25. The huddle is called by the clock at the middle of the lab (minute 10 of a 20-minute lab; minute 15 if you used `&min=30`). The code is on the teacher bar and in `#key`.

**At the huddle (about 5 minutes).** Say once: "Eyes up, screens half closed. Copy what I write. The blanks on your screen are in the same order." They cannot close the huddle until all seven are filled, then they enter the code.

| Frame | You write, in this order | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Core and shielding | 1. Core electrons: the electrons **inside** the outer ring. 2. Shielding: core electrons **cancel** part of the nucleus's pull. 3. Z_eff = Z minus the **core** electrons. | "Core means inside the outer ring. You were shading them." |
| 2 Sodium, worked | 4. Rings 2 + 8 + 1, total **11**. 5. Core = 2 + 8 = **10**. 6. Z_eff = 11 − 10 = **1**. 7. Pull = 1 ÷ 3² = **1/9**. KEEP: Z_eff = Z − core. | "Eleven protons, but it feels only plus one. That is the reading you took." Watch for: using +11 as the pull; counting the outer electron as core. |
| 3 Label the atom | Skip. The lab's own picture already labels it. | |
| 4 What is tested | Read, do not write: estimate Z_eff, count core electrons, say which of two atoms holds its outer electron harder. | "This is what the worksheet asks." |

**Then:** back to the lab: the mystery atom, then the paper worksheet.

**Numbers:** sodium comes from `chemistry/shared/zeff.js`, the same model as the page, so the board, the Huddle tab and the key agree. Pull is a classroom model number, not a measurement.
