# Slot 15 board script: the notes huddle (minute 21)

**Board:** chem board, `chemistry/whiteboard/index.html#lesson=zeff`. Four frames; the pictures are already drawn, you write into the blanks. Select an orange cover and press Delete to show the answer. Stencils to paste: the **Z_eff** shelf (Li, Na, K, Mg, Cl with the core shaded; the rule; the five moves; pull = Z_eff ÷ n²).

**Before minute 21:** say nothing. Students are on their computers in the Pull Meter. They have not been taught the words. No huddle at minute 3 or minute 25.

**Minute 21 (about 7 minutes).** Say once: "Eyes up, screens half closed. Copy what I write. Your Huddle tab has the same blanks, in the same order."

| Frame | You write, in this order | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Core and shielding | 1. Core electrons: the electrons **inside** the outer ring. 2. Shielding: core electrons **cancel** part of the nucleus's pull. 3. Z_eff = Z minus the **core** electrons. | "Core means inside the outer ring. You were marking them." |
| 2 Sodium, worked | Rings 2 + 8 + 1, total **11**. Core = 2 + 8 = **10**. Z_eff = 11 − 10 = **1**. Pull = 1 ÷ 3² = **1/9**. KEEP: Z_eff = Z − core. | "Eleven protons, but it feels only plus one." Watch for: using +11 as the pull; counting the outer electron as core. |
| 3 Label the atom | Label sodium: 1 nucleus charge **+11**. 2 the **core** rings hold **10** electrons. 3 the **outer** ring holds **1** electron. | Point at the amber rings, then the blue one. |
| 4 What is tested | Read, do not write: estimate Z_eff, count core electrons, say which of two atoms holds its outer electron harder. | "This is what the exit ticket asks." |

**Then:** they answer the three questions on their own screens (K1 to K3) and enter the code from the teacher panel (`#key` shows it). Back to the lab.

**Numbers:** sodium comes from `chemistry/shared/zeff.js`, the same model as the page, so the board, the Huddle tab and the key agree. Pull is a classroom model number, not a measurement.
