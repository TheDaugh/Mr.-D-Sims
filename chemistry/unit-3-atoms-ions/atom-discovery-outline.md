# Atom Discovery: Democritus to Dalton (Outline)

**Day 1, online. Core about 35 minutes (estimate, no class data yet). Day 2, hands-on.**
**Teacher key:** open `atom-discovery-key.html` (gate codes change daily)

## Big idea
Atoms were an argument for 2,200 years until someone weighed things. Fixed mass ratios, conserved mass and whole-number ratios were Dalton's evidence.

## Misconceptions targeted
- "Gold can be cut forever." Part 1 predict.
- "Any amounts of hydrogen and oxygen combine into water." Part 2 predict.
- "If no tool can cut an atom, it can't be cut."

## Sequence

| Part | Min | Students | Teacher |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cut It Forever | 0–8 | Predict, then halve a gold cube 75 times and watch atoms appear | Ask what a knife is made of |
| 2 Make Water | 8–18 | Spark trials, find 8 g O per 1 g H, see mass in equal mass out | Gate 1 huddle: class states the rule |
| 3 Two Compounds | 18–25 | Water against peroxide, find the 1 : 2 pattern | Walk the room |
| 4 Say It Right | 25–35 | Who said what, then what a solid ball cannot explain | Gate 2 huddle, then check stamps |
| ★ 5–7 Challenge | 35+ | Dalton's guess at formulas, nitrogen oxides, written response | Read the written response |

Day 2 (not built): static-charge stations with tape and balloons, picking up where the solid ball fails.

## Differentiation
Core and challenge tiers. Challenge is optional. No adaptive difficulty yet.

## Key features
- Predict locks before each sim.
- Wrong answers return a hint. The stamp counts first tries and retries.
- Gate codes change daily. The key page shows them.
