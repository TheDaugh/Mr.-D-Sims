# Atom Discovery: Democritus to Dalton (Outline)

**Day 1, online. One continuous lesson that runs to the 42-minute mark (Mon to Thu) or the bell (Friday). Day 2, hands-on.**
**Teacher view:** open `atom-discovery.html#key`. It is the real student page with every step open, the correct answers marked, huddle codes skipped, and locked mode off. The teacher bar at the top shows today's codes, the unlock-any-student code, and a clock simulator. The lock desk is under it. Codes and the periods are also on `../teacher.html`.

## Big idea
Atoms were an argument until someone weighed things. Fixed mass ratios, conserved mass and whole-number ratios were Dalton's evidence.

## Misconceptions targeted
- "Gold can be cut forever." Part 1 predict.
- "Any amounts of hydrogen and oxygen combine into water." Part 2 predict.
- "If no tool can cut an atom, it can't be cut."

## Sequence

| Step | Students | Teacher |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cut It Forever | Predict (with a one-sentence reason), then halve a gold cube 75 times and watch atoms appear | Ask what a knife is made of |
| 2 Make Water | Spark trials, find 8 g O per 1 g H, see mass in equal mass out, write the rule in their own words | Huddle 1: class states the rule |
| 3 Two Compounds | Water against peroxide, find the 1 : 2 pattern | Walk |
| 4 Say It Right | Who said what, then what a solid ball cannot explain | Huddle 2 |
| 5 Dalton's Guess | Test HO against H₂O on the oxygen atom's mass | Walk |
| 6 Nitrogen's Oxides | Three oxides, the 1 : 2 : 4 pattern, a fourth oxide | Walk |
| 7 Beyond Dalton | Written response: what a solid ball cannot explain | Read it on the final screen's summary |
| Keep practicing | Endless mass-ratio problems at the student's own level (1 to 4) until the end mark | Walk; the live view shows each student's step and level |
| Takeaways | Two thinkers, three laws, Dalton's five ideas, exit sentence. These fill in the notes | |
| Stamp and notes | Stamp (`ATOM-xxxx`), how far they got, and the notes to print, save as PDF, or download as `.tex` | Check stamps |

Nothing on the student page is timed. The lesson ends by the clock, not by finishing the parts: at the end mark an **End my guided notes** banner appears, and the stamp records which step the student reached. A student who finishes the parts early keeps practicing until the mark.

Day 2 (not built): static-charge stations with tape and balloons, where the solid ball fails.

## Notes
There is no written handout. The notes (**My notes** button, top right, and on the final screen) hold the Cold Open prediction and reason, the student's own sim data tables, the rule in their words, the takeaways and the exit sentence. They print, save as PDF, or download as LaTeX.

## Clock rules (shared/bell.js)
- Huddle codes are asked for only on weekdays 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM. After 3:00 PM, or at home, none. Today's codes are readable from 7:00 AM.
- Locked mode runs only during chemistry's period (opens 5 minutes early): until the 42-minute mark Mon to Thu, the whole period on Friday.
- Friday periods are 9:40 to 10:20 and 2:00 to 2:40. Mon to Thu: 10:40 and 1:50.

## Key features
- Predict locks first. Wrong answers return hints.
- Locked mode: fullscreen only. Exit 1 warns. From exit 2 the screen locks until you give that student a code from the lock desk, or the one override code that frees everyone.
- Scores are saved on the student's device (`mrd.profile.v1`) for the review portal. Each question carries a skill (`chem.definite-proportions`, etc.) that `portal/standards.json` maps to a standard.
