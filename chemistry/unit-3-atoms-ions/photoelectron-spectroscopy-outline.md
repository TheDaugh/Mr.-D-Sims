# Photoelectron spectroscopy (slot 11) · outline

**Page:** `photoelectron-spectroscopy.html` · **Data:** `chemistry/shared/pes-data.js` (sources: `pes-data-sources.md`)

**Target T9:** match a PES spectrum to an element; peak position = subshell energy, peak height = electron count; sketch a spectrum from a configuration.

**Elements:** H, He, Li, Ne, Mg, Ar. Only these have every subshell sourced. Na, S and P are out (see key).
