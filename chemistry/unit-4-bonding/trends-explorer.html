How to read this

Rings are shells. The outer edge is drawn to scale, so a bigger circle is a bigger atom. Dots on the outer ring are outer electrons. Inner electrons are not drawn.

SIZE is how big. LETS GO is how easily it gives up an outer electron. PULLS is how hard it pulls on an electron it shares. Noble gases rarely bond, so they have no PULLS bar.

Dotted gap in the table: other columns exist there. They are not part of this.
