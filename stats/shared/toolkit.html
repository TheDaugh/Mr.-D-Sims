Normal distribution toolkit

The table gives the area to the LEFT of z. For the right, subtract from 1.

The routine

  1. Picture. Sketch the curve. Shade the part the question asks for.
  2. z. Find z. Subtract the mean, then divide by the SD.
  3. Row. Find the row: the whole number and the first decimal of z.
  4. Column. Find the column: the second decimal of z.
  5. Area. Read the cell. It is the area to the LEFT. For the right, subtract from 1.
  6. Sentence. Say it in words: "About __% of ___ are ___."

Worked example (pretend numbers)

  1. Picture: Pretend quiz: mean 70, SD 10. What percent scored below 85? Shade the left of 85.
  2. z: z = (85 − 70) / 10 = 1.50
  3. Row: z = 1.50 → row 1.5
  4. Column: second decimal 0 → column .00
  5. Area: Where they cross: 0.9332. That is the area to the left.
  6. Sentence: About 93.32% of pretend scores are below 85. (Above 85: 1 − 0.9332 = 0.0668, about 6.68%.)
