  1. Choose which graph you want to read, then press New problem for a fresh motion.
  2. Press Play to watch the car, or drag the time slider. A black line on the graph follows the time.
  3. Read the question, work out the answer from the graph, and type it in. Press Check.
  4. After you check, the graph marks the part you needed: a shaded area, a highlighted slope, or a read-off point.
Theory

Learning to interpret motion graphs is an important skill in physics. The most important step is to read the vertical axis carefully. Is it a velocity graph or a displacement graph?

If it is a displacement graph:

  • The vertical value is how far the object is from the start.
  • The slope represents the velocity. A steeper line is a faster object, and a downward slope is motion backwards.

If it is a velocity graph:

  • The velocity can be read directly from the vertical axis.
  • The slope represents the acceleration.
  • The area under the graph represents the distance travelled. Area below the axis counts as negative, because the car is going backwards.
