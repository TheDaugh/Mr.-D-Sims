# Newton's Second Law: Day 2 Outline

**Format:** online guided lesson. Open `newtons-second-law.html`. Add `#key` for the teacher key on every screen. Codes, answers and the report reader are on `teacher.html`.

## Big idea
Net force sets acceleration: a = F/m. Same push, more mass, less acceleration. A constant net force gives a constant acceleration, not a constant speed.

## Misconception targeted
"A bigger force means a bigger speed." (Force changes speed; it does not set it.)

## Sequence
1. **Warm-up.** Five items set a silent starting level, 1 to 4.
2. **Predict.** Three locked predictions with confidence and a written reason: v–t graph under a steady push, doubling the force, doubling the mass.
3. **Investigate.** One cart track, four parts, each with a job.
   - A: five fan forces. Find the slope of a against F.
   - B: five masses at one force. Find m × a.
   - C: rough track. Choose the fan force that gives a target acceleration (friction counts).
   - D: hanging mass over a pulley. Why the answer is never g.
4. **Huddle 1.** Teacher code. Predictions against evidence. Student writes the rule.
5. **Name it.** Explanation in three registers, the law as a fill-in, free-body diagrams plus net-force direction, vocabulary in the student's words, the misconception.
6. **Try it.** Six skills, four levels each, routed up or down by performance.
7. **Check.** Huddle 2 code. The same three questions for all, plus reflection.
8. **Notes.** Endless practice until the end mark, then stamp and report code. Print as PDF or download LaTeX.

## Differentiation
Level 1 reads patterns in tables. Level 4 is AP and first-year college: vector form, systems, tension, two changes at once. Numbers are seeded per student.

## Teacher tools
Daily codes, per-student answers with `#key`, a Preview level control, and a report-code table reader that recognizes both lessons.
