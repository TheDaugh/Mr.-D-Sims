1 · Predict

A hockey puck (0.16 kg) is struck once and slides across the ice. Once it leaves the stick, nothing else pushes it. Does it stop on its own?

Once locked, it stays. The point is to compare what you thought with what happens.

4 · Name it

The rule you wrote has a formal name. This is Newton’s First Law:

An object keeps its velocity, both speed and direction, unless a net external force acts on it.

In symbols, when ΣF = 0 the velocity does not change. Zero velocity counts too: a book at rest stays at rest.

Match the wording above. Spacing and capital letters do not matter.
