# Free-Body Diagrams and Net Force: Slot 4 Outline

**Format:** online guided lesson. Open `fbd-net-force.html`; add `#key` for the teacher key on every tab. The projector page is `fbd-net-force-projector.html`. Codes and the report reader are on `teacher.html`.

## Big idea
A free-body diagram shows every force on one object, one arrow each. The net force is what is left after the arrows on a line cancel. Net force sets the change in motion, not the motion.

## Sequence
1. **Warm-up.** Vectors and Proportions Check, six items. Direction misses get the arrow table first; six right flags the slot 20 incline work.
2. **Predict, Explore.** Three one-try predictions, then the force catalog.
3. **Model problems.** Five models on one six-move rail: watch two, work two with Mr. D, then do one alone. Each opens with a predict.
4. **Huddle (three minutes).** Three diagram checks give a C, M, X or S code. Read the class split on the projector page, then send students back.
5. **Build.** Six source and catalog questions, then rest, steady motion, speeding up, then net force.
6. **Practice, Check.** Six hidden bands. A clean first try moves up, one miss holds, two drop with the reasoning shown. Build stops at band 4; bands 5 and 6 live here. Sims open inside the lesson, only for the band they serve.
7. **Wrap-up.** Journal, notes, stamp.

## Misconceptions targeted
"Motion needs a force" (the extra arrow). "N always equals W." "Tension equals the hanging weight."

## Worksheet
Six situations, scored 0 to 3, scale printed on it.
